0. CORE ARCHITECTURE & CACHING STRATEGY
=============================================================================
- Brand Model: Data contains "IM3" and "3ID". "IOH" is always the sum of IM3 + 3ID.
- Caching: Reports are expensive. Backend caches payloads in-memory.
  - Marker: `dt_id_snapshot` (for highlevel) and `as_of_dt` (for data rev).
  - Web & Mobile check `/api/reports/data-version` every 2 mins.
  - Cache Key: `report_type|data_version|circle|brand|scope_type|scope_value`.
  - When BigQuery updates, `data_version` changes, Cache Key changes -> Auto-rebuilds instantly.

1. COMPREHENSIVE REPORT
=============================================================================
**Data Source**: `rham_mkt_highlevel_mtd` (current month), `rham_mkt_highlevel_mth` (closed months history).
**Logic**:
- Timeframes: LLMTD (mid-2), LMTD (mid-1), MTD (mid), LYTD (mid-100), YTD (cumulative).
- Metric Types:
  * Flow Metrics (Revenue, Gross Add, Usage): YTD = SUM(Jan..MTD).
  * Snapshot Metrics (Site, RGU, VLR, Outlets): YTD = point-in-time (MTD current vs MTD last year).
- Computed Ratios: Computed POST-aggregation. e.g., ARPU = (Rev Prepaid MTD) / (RGU 90D MTD).
**UI/UX**:
- Brand-Banded Table: 1st column is "Metric" (Sticky Left, z-index 3).
- 9 Columns per Brand (IOH / IM3 / 3ID): LLMTD, LMTD, MTD, Var, %, LYTD, YTD, Var YoY, YoY%.
- Zebra rows, Tints: Var uses Steel Blue (#DAE5EE), Growth uses Sand Gold (#F2EAD2).
- Font: tabular-nums for digits. Arrows: ▲ (green #13A58F) / ▼ (red #E26A6A).

=============================================================================
2. PREPAID DATA REVENUE
=============================================================================
**Data Source**: `rham_site_data_rev_channel_mtd` & `_mth` (Channel), `_price_slab_mtd` (Slabs).
**Logic**:
- Grouped by `channel_group` (Organic, Digital Distribution, MOBO Trade) and `channel_detail` (APPS, UMB, etc).
- 3 Chunks: Total Channel, ATL (Non CVM), BTL (CVM).
- Ratios: Channel Mix % vs Total Data Rev, ATL vs BTL %, ATL/BTL multiplier (e.g. 1.8x).
- Price Slab: 15 fixed ordered slabs (0-10k, 10-20k, ..., above 1Mn). Data is NOT channel-aware (rendered as Total/IM3/3ID).
**UI/UX**:
- Header: Headline IOH Revenue + MoM Growth.
- Expandable Rows (Accordion): Channel group (e.g., ORGANIC) can be tapped to expand `channel_detail` rows (indented 22px).
- Columns (frozen Channel name): LLMTD, LMTD, MTD, Var, %, LYTD, YTD, Var YoY, YoY%, Contrib %.

=============================================================================
3. PRODUCT DEMAND
=============================================================================
**Data Source**: `rham_site_data_rev_product_mtd` & `_mth`.
**Logic**:
- Grouped by `quota_slab` (8 bins) x `validity_slab` (7 bins) x `channel_group`.
- Generates `slab_scopes` for: Total, Organic, Digital, Trade. Inside each scope -> quota slab tables, validity slab tables, and Top 10 Products table.
- Heatmap Matrix: Quota (Y-axis) x Validity (X-axis). 
**UI/UX**:
- Top Panel: Slab tables (Quota / Validity toggle). Rendered identical to Data Revenue tables (9 columns). Includes Top Products table.
- Bottom Panel: 
  * Rank Legend (#1 Red, #2 Magenta, #3 Yellow).
  * Revenue Heatmap (Teal opacity based on value/Max). Rank badges overlay the cells.
  * Growth MoM% Heatmap (Diverging: Red for negative, Teal for positive).

=============================================================================
4. SITE PERFORMANCE
=============================================================================
**Data Source**: `rham_site_wise_addr_java` (attributes), `_addr_java_perf` (LMTD/MTD/Growth precomputed per brand), `_monthly` (6-month history), `rham_site_ga_daily` (Funnel).
**Logic**:
- Search by GPS (Nearest 10) or Text (Site ID / Name).
- Detail fetches: Attributes, KPI (MTD), Funnel (M1S->M2S->P1M->P2M->GA), Network (2G/4G/5G Usage/Traffic/Device), Availability (Uptime/Downtime), FWA.
**UI/UX**:
- 2 Main Views: Snapshot (MTD metrics) & Full Month (6-month history).
- Snapshot Tables: `Metric | LMTD | MTD | Growth` for selected Brand.
- Full Month Tables: `Metric | M1 | M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6` (X-axis is month_id, Y-axis is metric).
- Top Products, Price Slabs, and Channel Revenue are filtered specific to the Site ID.
